A simple structure for your priorities

The 1-3-5 Rule organizes your plan around one main task, three medium tasks, and five small tasks.

1 big task

Your main focus.

3 medium tasks

Additional tasks that support the focus.

5 small tasks

Shorter tasks that fit around larger work.

Main Focus: Efficiency — maximum productivity with minimal wasted time.

How to apply the 1-3-5 Rule

Use these steps to build a daily or weekly 1-3-5 to-do list.

Step 1: List your tasks

At the beginning of each week, list the tasks you want to get done. Include tasks carried over from last week and regular tasks. Make each task concrete and actionable.

Step 2: Categorize each task

Categorize each task as big, medium, or small.

Big: about 3–4 hours

Medium: about 1–2 hours

Small: less than 30 minutes to 1 hour

Step 3: Plot your 1-3-5 list

Plot the tasks into a daily 1-3-5 to-do list. Plan it the night before, in the morning, or create the list for the entire week in advance.

Step 4: Match the order to your energy

Choose the order that fits your energy and focus. You can tackle the biggest task first when that works for you, or designate times for each task category based on your energy levels.

Additional tip: unexpected work

Schedule unexpected work after completing the 1-3-5 list for the day, or move it to the next day or week as part of your 1-3-5 list.
