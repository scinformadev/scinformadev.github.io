The distinction

Urgent and important describe different reasons a task deserves attention.

module2-lesson-1-1.png

Two considerations

When considering a task, ask whether it requires immediate attention or helps you achieve long-term goals.

Urgent tasks

Urgent tasks require immediate attention and must be done now.

Urgent task

Requires immediate attention. It must be done now and has clear consequences if it is not completed within a certain timeline. It is a task you can’t avoid.

Immediate attention

Urgent work is connected to a clear timeline and pressure to respond.

Clear consequences

Delaying the task can create consequences within the relevant timeline.

Important tasks

Important tasks help you achieve long-term goals. Flip each card to explore what makes work important.

Important task

Does not require immediate attention but helps you achieve long-term goals. It contributes significantly to your success, growth, or well-being and focuses on prevention, improvement, or long-term planning.

From framework to tools

Urgency and importance give you a framework for deciding what deserves attention. The next lessons turn that framework into practical tools.

1-3-5 Rule

Build a manageable daily or weekly task list with one big task, three medium tasks, and five small tasks.

Eisenhower Matrix

Decide what to do, schedule, delegate, or eliminate by considering urgency and importance together.

80/20 Rule

Focus on the vital few activities that are most likely to create the greatest results.
