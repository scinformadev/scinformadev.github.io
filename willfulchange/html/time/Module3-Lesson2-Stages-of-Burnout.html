The progression

Burnout may develop gradually and progress through several stages. The stages are a framework for noticing patterns, not a prediction that every person will experience each one.

Stages of burnout

The stages are the honeymoon phase, onset of stress, chronic stress, burnout, and chronic burnout.

People may move through stages differently. Severity and duration can vary, and early awareness creates an opportunity to seek support and respond.

Stages of burnout

Move through the stages to review how burnout may develop.

Honeymoon phase

A person feels motivated, energetic, and dedicated to their work.

Onset of stress

The person begins feeling stress as work hours, responsibilities, or pressure increase.

Chronic stress

Stress continues for a longer period. The person may feel overwhelmed and fatigued, and physical symptoms may arise.

Burnout

The person is physically, emotionally, and mentally exhausted. Interest and productivity may decline, and cynicism may increase.

Chronic burnout

When burnout is not addressed, functioning at work and home may become significantly impaired, and health concerns may worsen.
