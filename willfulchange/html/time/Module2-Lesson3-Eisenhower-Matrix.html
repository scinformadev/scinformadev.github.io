A framework for better decisions

The Eisenhower Matrix compares each task’s urgency and importance so you can focus on the right action—not simply the loudest request.

  • Urgency: Does this need immediate attention?
  • Importance: Does this support your role, goals, or long-term results?
  • Outcome: Decide whether to do, schedule, delegate, or eliminate it.

How to apply the Eisenhower Matrix

Use this four-step process to turn a full task list into clear next actions.

Step 1: List tasks

Write down all tasks you need to complete. This can be done at the beginning of each week.

Step 2: Assess urgency and importance

Assess both dimensions for each task. Be clear about the expectations of your role and your overall goals when deciding what is important.

Step 3: Place tasks in quadrants

Place each task in the corresponding quadrant based on its urgency and importance.

Step 4: Take action

Use the quadrant to choose the corresponding next action.

Do: urgent and important tasks

Schedule: important but not urgent tasks

Delegate: urgent but not important tasks

Eliminate: tasks that are neither urgent nor important

Start with clarity

Your role expectations and overall goals help you judge importance consistently.
