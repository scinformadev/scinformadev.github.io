Common workload challenges

You may have thought of something specific to your role. Here are a few challenges many people experience when balancing their workload.

Task prioritization

Deciding which tasks deserve your attention first when several responsibilities compete for your time.

Setting boundaries

Communicating your availability, capacity, and limits so your workload remains manageable.

External factors: organizational culture

Recognizing how workplace expectations, norms, and systems can influence workload and capacity.

Saying no and taking regular breaks

Declining requests that exceed your capacity and pausing to protect your focus and energy.
