Brainstorming

Brainstorming is mostly used in the ideation phase of problem-solving.

Brainstorming is a design method that teams use to generate ideas to solve clearly defined problems. In controlled conditions and a free-thinking environment, teams approach a problem through questions such as “How might we…?” They produce a vast array of ideas and draw links between them to find potential solutions.

How to use brainstorming

Step 1

Clearly outline the problem or objective and define your desired goal.

Step 2

Create a diverse group.

  • Diverse representation
  • Inclusive environment
  • Equitable participation
  • Cultural sensitivity

Step 3

Set a time limit. Depending on the complexity of the problem, 15–60 minutes is a good place to start.

Step 4

Begin the session with thought-provoking questions related to the problem you want to solve.

  • How might we…?
  • If we could solve…
  • If you could change…
