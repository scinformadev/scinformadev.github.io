Decision-making

In psychology, decision-making is regarded as the cognitive process resulting in the selection of a belief or a course of action among several possible alternative options. It is a reasoning process based on assumptions about the values, preferences, and beliefs of the decision maker.

Decision-making factors to consider

The decision-making process

  1. 1

    Identifying the challenge

    In this step, the manager discovers an issue and determines the circumstances that led to the situation.

  2. 2

    Devising solutions

    After learning more information about the case, the manager creates one or several possible solutions.

  3. 3

    Weighing options

    The manager analyzes the advantages and disadvantages of each option and explores alternative solutions if needed.

  4. 4

    Making a choice

    Once a thorough assessment takes place, the manager makes a final decision about what action to take.

  5. 5

    Informing others of the decision

    The manager informs employees of the decision and explains how the decision influences the workplace.
