Socratic method

The Socratic method, developed by the ancient Greek philosopher Socrates, is a form of cooperative argumentative dialogue between individuals, based on asking and answering questions to stimulate critical thinking and illuminate ideas.

Socratic method in process

Scenario: A staff member consistently arrives late to team meetings.

Step 1: Clarify the goal

Determine the purpose of using the Socratic method: understand the reasons behind the staff member’s consistent lateness and find a solution that addresses the issue.

Step 2: Establish a constructive dialogue

Approach the staff member in a non-confrontational manner, expressing concern and a desire to understand their perspective.

Example

“I’ve noticed that you’ve been arriving late to our team meetings consistently. Can we discuss what might be causing this?”

Step 3: Ask open-ended questions

Encourage the staff member to think critically about their behavior and its impact.

Example

“What factors do you think are contributing to your lateness? How do you think your lateness affects the team?”

Step 4: Probe for reasons and evidence

Dig deeper into the staff member’s responses to uncover underlying reasons for their behavior.

Example

“You mentioned traffic as a reason for your lateness. Have you considered leaving earlier or using alternate routes?”

Step 5: Challenge assumptions and encourage reflection

Encourage the staff member to reflect on their actions and consider alternative perspectives.

Example

“Do you think arriving late sends a message to the team about your commitment? How do you think we can ensure everyone is respecting each other’s time?”

Step 6: Evaluate solutions and generate alternatives

Collaborate with the staff member to find solutions that address the root cause of the lateness.

Example

“What do you think would help you arrive on time consistently? Would a different meeting time or format be more suitable?”

Step 7: Reach a conclusion

Summarize the discussion and agree on a plan of action.

Example

“Based on our discussion, it seems that adjusting your schedule and finding alternative routes could help. Let’s try that and revisit if it improves your punctuality.”

Step 8: Follow up

Monitor the staff member’s progress and provide support as needed.

Example

“I noticed you’ve been more punctual recently. How are the changes working for you? Is there anything else we can do to support you?”
