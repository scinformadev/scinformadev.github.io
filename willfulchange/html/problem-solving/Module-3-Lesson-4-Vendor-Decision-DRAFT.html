Your decision briefing

Your organization has a $60,000 budget for leadership development training. The goal is to improve leadership effectiveness, employee engagement, and organizational performance.

Three proposals. One vendor choice.

Compare the vendors, explore a weighted decision matrix, and review what your choice offers and leaves uncertain.

The organization’s priority

Comprehensive training is important. The organization is willing to invest more when the overall benefit outweighs the cost.

Use the Back and Next buttons to move through the exercise. Select the preset choices to reveal feedback; there are no written responses.

Compare the three proposals

All three vendors fit within the $60,000 budget. Compare cost, duration, program features, and the strength of each vendor’s evidence.

Vendor A

$50,000 · 6 months

  • Personalized coaching, online modules, in-person workshops
  • Track record with similar organizations; high satisfaction
  • Flexible schedule and strong reputation

Vendor B

$40,000 · 4 months

  • Online modules, virtual workshops, peer discussions
  • Practical skills focus and good online platform
  • Moderate satisfaction; less brand recognition

Vendor C

$55,000 · 8 months

  • Comprehensive curriculum, in-person workshops, ongoing support
  • Innovative and interactive methods; excellent customer reviews
