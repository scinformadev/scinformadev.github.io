Opening reflection question

Think of a recent problem

Describe a problem you’ve encountered recently and share details around the skills or framework you used to approach solving the problem?

Review the sample responses on the next slide. As you read, consider how you approached a problem of your own.

Sample responses

These examples show two ways a person might describe a recent challenge. You will explore the methods in the course.

Problem-solving example

Problem: Our team kept missing project deadlines.

Approach: I used listening and collaboration. I spoke with the people involved to understand what was getting in the way. Together, we identified a change to try and watched whether it helped.

Decision-making example

Problem: Our team needed to choose between two possible dates for an event.

Approach: I used careful comparison and communication. I considered how each date would affect the people involved, then chose one and explained the reason to the team.

Reflect: Does your recent experience sound more like solving a problem, making a decision, or both?

Carry your reflection into the course

Your experience is a starting point
