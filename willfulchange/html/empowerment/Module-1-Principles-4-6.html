4) Provide the Right Resources and Level of Authority

Equip the employee to lead the assignment without unnecessary oversight.

5) Establish Clear Communication Channels

Clear communication channels make it easier for employees to ask questions, share progress, and raise concerns before they affect the work.

6) Allow for Failure

Allowing room for failure encourages experimentation and empowers employees to try new approaches.
