Imagine you’re facing a challenging project, and you need to delegate tasks to your team. What factors would you consider in deciding which tasks to delegate and to whom?

Delegation considerations

You may have thought of something specific to your role. Here are a few factors to consider when deciding which tasks to delegate and to whom.

Current skills

Consider whether the task aligns with the team member’s current skills.

Professional goals

Consider whether the task aligns with the team member’s professional goals.

Development opportunity

Consider whether the task could be a stretch project that develops an area of weakness.

Task and person fit

Consider which team member is best suited for the task based on these factors.
