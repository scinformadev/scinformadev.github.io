Delegation defined

Begin with what delegation means before exploring the principles.

The process of distributing and entrusting work to another person. In leadership, it involves a manager aiming to efficiently distribute work, decision-making, and responsibility to subordinate workers in an organization.

Benefits of delegation

Delegation can benefit both leaders and team members.

Free up time to focus on higher-value activities

Keeps employees engaged with greater autonomy

It enhances creativity and innovation by leveraging diverse skills

Boosts morale & motivation

Increases Productivity

Reduce Employee Turnover

Improves trust & communication
