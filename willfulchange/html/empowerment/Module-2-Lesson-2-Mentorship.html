Strategies for Nurturing Leadership Qualities

Mentorship is a dynamic and reciprocal relationship between an experienced and knowledgeable individual (mentor) and a less experienced or knowledgeable individual (mentee) aimed at fostering the mentee’s personal and professional growth.

Forms of Mentorship

  • Formal Arrangement
  • Informal Arrangement

Mentorship Settings

  • Workplace
  • Educational Institute
  • Professional Association

Mentorship Expectations

  • Guidance
  • Support
  • Advice
  • Knowledge Transfer

5 Stages of a Mentoring Relationship

Move through the carousel to review each stage.

Stage 1: Initiation/Introduction

This is the initial phase where the mentor and mentee get to know each other and establish the foundation for their mentoring relationship.

Stage 2: Cultivation/Relationship Building

This stage focuses on building rapport, trust, and a strong relationship between the mentor and mentee.

Stage 3: Agreement/Goal Setting

In this stage, the mentor and mentee collaborate to set specific goals and objectives for the mentoring relationship.

Stage 4: Implementation/Action

This stage involves actively working toward the established goals and implementing the strategies outlined in the goal-setting stage.

Stage 5: Closure/Transition

The closure stage marks the end of the formal mentoring relationship. It involves reflection on achievements, lessons learned, and potential next steps.
