7) Be Patient

IMPORTANT

You might be tempted to refrain from delegating certain tasks knowing that you can get them done faster, but be patient with your employees.

“Patience is not passive waiting. Patience is active acceptance of the process required to attain your goals and dreams.”

8) Deliver (and ask for) Feedback

Flip each card to review how to give feedback and how to ask for feedback.

9) Give Credit Where It’s Due

IMPORTANT

After you've delegated tasks and they've been seen through to completion, credit those who achieved the work. The more you thank and credit those you've delegated work to, the more likely it is they will want to help you on other projects in the future.

Reflection

Consider how recognizing another person's contribution can affect their willingness to help with future projects.
